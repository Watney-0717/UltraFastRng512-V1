4. ROOT-CAUSE DECISION
----------------------
No new high-confidence root cause has been identified that justifies changing V1.
The earlier sign/dep2 defect was caused by MC pair formation from coupled consecutive fanout rows; the selected odd-stride cache geometry separates those physical source rows while preserving sequential MC consumption.

The remaining visible tail/moment differences are downstream Normal-approximation effects rather than the old pair-geometry defect. The V1 Normal-LUT tail refinement already reduced the 3-sigma relative error to approximately +0.375% and the 4-sigma relative error to approximately -1.667% in the 134M-pair audit.
